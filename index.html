How I work

My process, step by step

This is how I take a company from no list to signed clients.

STEP 1

Define the target

I start with the ideal customer profile: industry, company size, buyer roles and the problems the product solves.

STEP 2

Build the lead list

I research and segment contacts in tools like Apollo and Sales Navigator, so every message goes to the right person.

20,000+ contact database built
STEP 3

Run the outreach

I write cold email sequences, send LinkedIn messages and make calls, then adjust the copy based on reply rates.

12%+ reply rate
STEP 4

Qualify and book

I score leads by engagement and fit, follow up through the CRM, and book meetings with the ones ready to talk.

+45% MQL to SQL
STEP 5

Close the deal

I run discovery, handle objections and negotiate the contract, then stay in touch after the sale for repeat business.

$150K+ closed as an AE
STEP 6

Report and improve

I keep the CRM clean, track KPIs and review the funnel to see what to change.

30% team performance lift
